Is it worth it?

For this question, yes. Going from 50 to 200 rows took the answer from 51 of 52 rows (with the list cut short) to all 138 correct. The extra prompt is about 730 tokens on the largest step, which is small against a 20k window. The cost shows up in the output: the model writes the whole table itself, about 1,000 more tokens. show_table would remove that cost, but the model still doesn't call it, so for now the cap is the lever that works.
